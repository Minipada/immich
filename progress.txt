Issue #5: Silence-aligned chunking, resumption, and progressive transcripts

Replaced the tracer bullet's whole-file extraction with a chunked pipeline
that survives interruption and whose partial output is readable while the
job is still running. All ten acceptance criteria are covered.

Chunking (server/src/utils/transcription.ts, media.repository.ts):
- Audio is decoded once to raw 16 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM, with ffmpeg's
  `silencedetect` filter riding along on the same decode pass. A second
  pass over a long file costs as much as the first, so detection is not
  worth separating.
- `planChunks` walks from zero in steps of the target duration and moves
  each boundary to the middle of the nearest silence within a +/-5s window,
  clamping the silence to the window first. The midpoint is the point
  furthest from speech on either side, which leaves the most room for the
  imprecision in where detection places its edges. No qualifying silence
  means a hard cut at the target.
- Deterministic by construction: it sorts its input and breaks distance
  ties toward the earlier candidate, so boundaries do not depend on the
  order ffmpeg happened to report silences in. A unit test asserts a
  reversed silence list plans identically.
- The loop stops splitting once the remainder fits in one chunk, so the
  last chunk absorbs the tail rather than leaving a sliver behind it.
- A silence still open at end of stream is reported as ending at Infinity
  and clamped by the planner, rather than being dropped.

Resumption (transcript.repository.ts, asset-job-status.table.ts):
- `appendChunk` writes a chunk's segments and the progress marker it
  advances to in a single transaction. Splitting them would let a crash in
  between leave rows for a chunk that is then re-run.
- Progress lives in a new `asset_job_status.transcriptionProgressMs`
  column (migration 1785419018640) as a millisecond offset, not a chunk
  index. Indices are not stable across runs: change the chunk size or the
  silence thresholds between a failure and its retry and a given index
  denotes a different region.
- `planResume` drops chunks the offset already covers and clips the chunk
  that straddles it back to the offset. Skipping to the next boundary
  instead — the literal reading of "start at the first boundary at or
  beyond the offset" — would silently drop the audio between the offset and
  that boundary whenever re-planning moved it. Clipping is the only
  version that produces neither duplicates nor gaps; a remainder under
  0.25s is dropped because it cannot hold a word.
- A chunk that VAD finds no speech in still advances progress, so it is not
  replayed on every resume.
- `reset` (fresh run) and `deleteAll` (forced re-queue) clear segments and
  the marker together.

Progressive display:
- `getStatus` reads progress without gating on completion. Three states,
  each with a consumer: never started drives the queue-all stream and the
  fresh-run reset; in flight drives the resume planner and keeps the web
  client polling; complete stops both.
- The captions endpoint carries the state in WebVTT `NOTE` blocks. Players
  ignore notes, so a partial transcript renders as a caption track exactly
  like a finished one, and no new endpoint or DTO was needed.
- The web player refetches the track every 15s into a blob URL while the
  video is open, reasserting `mode = 'showing'` on each load because a
  track element resets to disabled when its source is replaced. It stops
  once the transcript is marked complete.

Inference timeout (machine-learning.repository.ts):
- `predict` gained an optional `timeout`, supplied only by transcription,
  so every other caller keeps waiting indefinitely as before.
- The value is `chunkDuration * timeoutMultiplier`, defaulting to 30x, with
  a 60s floor so first-use model loading (which downloads ~500 MB) is not
  mistaken for a hang. Against the 3.5x worst case measured in issue #4's
  performance gate, 30x is roughly an order of magnitude of headroom.
- Failover is unchanged. Because progress is persisted per chunk, a timeout
  costs at most one chunk of duplicated inference.

ML side: chunks arrive as raw PCM rather than a WAV container. At chunk
scale compression buys nothing, while raw removes a decode dependency and
is byte-identical to what faster-whisper feeds its encoder. The transcriber
converts int16 to normalised float32 and ignores a trailing odd byte, which
cannot form a sample.

Config: `machineLearning.transcription` gained `chunkDuration` (30s,
5..600) and `timeoutMultiplier` (30, min 1). OpenAPI spec and the
TypeScript SDK client were regenerated.

Tests:
- 22 unit tests on the planners covering short audio, no silences, silence
  exactly spanning the target, a silence overrunning the window, an
  unterminated trailing silence, determinism, and gap/overlap-free
  coverage; plus byte-range, duration and timeout maths.
- 16 unit tests on the service covering chunk splitting at silences,
  segment time offsetting, timeout scaling, resumption, zero-speech
  progress, and the already-complete no-op.
- 9 medium tests against a real Postgres covering per-chunk atomicity (a
  failing insert leaves neither partial rows nor advanced progress), the
  three status states, reset/deleteAll, and duplicate-free resumption both
  with a stable boundary set and with one that changed between runs.
- Full server unit suite green (2288 passed). ML suite green (108 passed).
  Server lint/format/typecheck and web lint/format/svelte-check green.

Not done / caveats:
- The medium-test harness could not start its own database here:
  testcontainers fails against this host's Docker 29.5 with "Log stream
  ended", on a clean checkout as much as on this branch. The medium tests
  were run against a manually started `ghcr.io/immich-app/postgres` with
  `IMMICH_TEST_POSTGRES_URL` pointed at it, and all 9 new tests pass. Two
  unrelated medium suites (exif, workflow-core-plugin) fail in this
  environment for want of exiftool and a built plugin-core.
- The Dart SDK was not regenerated: `openapi-generator-cli` is unavailable
  here, and `mobile/openapi` already lacked `TranscriptionConfig` from the
  tracer bullet, so this branch does not make it any staler.
- `src/queries/*.sql` was not regenerated either, matching what the tracer
  bullet did; those snapshots are already stale for the transcription
  repositories.
- Chunking is planned but end-to-end behaviour on a real long video was not
  measured. Issue #4 noted peak RSS of ~1.5 GiB on a 15-minute video as the
  argument for chunking; confirming the new ceiling is worth a follow-up.
- Silence thresholds (-30 dBFS, 0.25s) and the +/-5s search window are
  constants, not configuration. They are the knobs most likely to need
  tuning on unusual material.
